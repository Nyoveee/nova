Game Concept:
Ichorus is a 1st Person Movement Shooter following Ichorus the main character, fighting back a malicious group using ichor of gods to create automatons.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Controls:

WASD - Moving
Spacebar - Jump
Shift - Dash
Mouse & LMB - Aim & Shoot
F -> F to use player's ultimate.
E -> E to interact with the objects and environment.
P -> Pause

Cheat codes 
P -> Skip Cutscene Video in Level One
K to commit sudoku
L to toggle speed hack
O to skip quest (level 1 only)
I to take 30 damage
U to gain 10 health
Y to gain 17 SP

Throwing: 
Hold Right -> To arm the weapon.
When Right Click if fully charged, press left click to throw. 

Ichor Collection mechanic: Weapon uses ammunition fire. When weapon runs out of ammo, player must collect ichor to restore ammunition. Ichor is dropped from enemy units. 
Collecting ichor also results in healing, and excess results in SP gain. 

Weapon Throwing Mechanics: 
Weapon is thrown forwards, when it hits a target, environment or after a while it will boomerang back to the player. The thrown weapon collects ichor on the way. Weapon deals damages to enemies. Weapon deals increase damage when the ammo is low. 

Execute: 
When the enemy is low on health, enemy can be executed by the weapon throw. 

Weapon Ultimate:
When the weapon collects enough ichor, the player can use the ultimate. The ultimate is a energy ball the fires in a direction, dealing massive Area of Effect Damage. 

Objective:
Complete the objectives until the player is able to open the door in the ground and jump instead it.

Questline: Player has a questline, follow the quest line to complete the game.
